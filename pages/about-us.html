Who we are

About Angkor Journey

Our Story

Founded in Cambodia. Built on Belonging.

Angkor Journey was founded in 2014 by a group of Cambodian travel enthusiasts who believed that the best guide to any place is someone who loves it. We started with a single sunrise tour of Angkor Wat and a handful of handwritten itineraries.

Over a decade later, we've helped thousands of travelers experience Cambodia in ways that leave a lasting impression — not just a photo album. Our guides are locals. Our partners are community-owned. Our impact stays in Cambodia.

We're proud to be a travel agency that genuinely cares about the places and people we show the world.

Our Mission

To share Cambodia's wonder responsibly, authentically, and unforgettably.

We believe travel done well creates understanding. We exist to help travelers connect with Cambodia's culture, people, and landscapes in ways that benefit both visitor and community — leaving the country better than we found it.
